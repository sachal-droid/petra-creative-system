# Deck

Load `petra:standards`, then run `petra:strategy` (or reuse its Locked Brief). A deck for a buyer runs the trade-facing variant. Then follow this.

## Brief additions

- Audience and setting: buyer meeting, line review submission read without a presenter, investor, internal, licensing partner
- Length target and whether it is presented or sent
- Numbers available: doors, velocity, ratings, revenue, growth, social, with the owner of each figure (who confirms it)
- Existing decks to build on, and the slide master or brand template if one exists
- The ask: what the deck must get a yes to

## Specialist skills

Use when installed: `strategy-finishing` (deck narrative, emotional and rational balance, deck design), `pptx` (production), `art-director` (slide composition review). Imagery comes from `petra:images` rules.

## Structures

Retail sell-in (buyer or line review). Two acts, short, no repetition, no claims appendix:
1. Brand intro: meet the brand, founder story in two lines, vision and purpose, the brand today (proof in numbers)
2. The case: category disruption (what is broken on shelf), growth drivers, the retailer's P&L for this range (SRP, margin, velocity assumptions), competitors and why this brand wins, joint category plan (assortment, displays, launch support, replenishment), the ask

Brand deck: who we are, what we make, who buys it and why, proof, where we are going, how to work with us.

Pitch or strategy deck: problem, insight, proposition, the idea, how it works, proof it will work, plan, ask.

Licensing deck: the character or brand, its audience and reach, the world and style guide, categories open, partner proof, terms outline.

## Directions

Each Direction Card adds: the narrative arc in one line per act, the visual system (layout grid, type scale, imagery style, chart style), and a drawn title slide and one content slide.

## Render plan (Gate 2)

Slide-by-slide outline with the headline sentence of each slide, the evidence on it, and the imagery it needs (generated or supplied). Generated imagery rows carry model, ratio (16:9 or 1:1 for inset), references, prompt summary. Total generations, preflighted cost, balance. Wait for approval.

## Production

1. Write every slide headline as a full sentence that carries the argument. A reader skimming only headlines gets the whole case.
2. Confirm every number with the user before it is typed. Each figure carries its source and date in the notes.
3. Generate imagery, approve, then build the .pptx with the brand's type and palette. Charts are native, not images.
4. One idea per slide. Cut any slide that repeats an earlier one.

## Quality check additions

- Numbers audit: every figure matches the confirmed list; no distribution implied that the brand does not have; dates present
- Headline test: headlines alone tell the story in order
- Trade copy rules met; flagged claims resolved
- Build: fonts embedded or substituted cleanly, images at 2K or less, file opens in PowerPoint and Keynote, speaker notes where the deck is presented

## Delivery

.pptx at 16:9, a PDF export when the deck is sent rather than presented, hosted URL per generated image, job record.
